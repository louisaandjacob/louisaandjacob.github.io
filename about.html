Programme

Friday — 04/09/2026

16:00-17.30 — Walking tour of Copenhagen

  • Nerd Tours has promised to give us a unique introduction to Copenhagen. The tour will start at Torvegade 75 at 16.00. We would be very happy if those planning to attend could help contribute: it costs 125 DKK per person, which you can transfer to either Jacob or Louisa. Feel free to bring a beer or other tasty liquids for the walk!

18:00-Late - Street food & drinks

  • We have booked tables at Broens Gadekøkken, just across the bridge from Nyhavn or a few minutes’ walk from Christianshavns Station. We will buy the first round of pitchers, but food and additional drinks are at your own expense.

Saturday — 05/09/2026

10:30–11:00 — Ceremony

  • We kick off the day with ten romantic minutes at Copenhagen City Hall, where we get the paperwork out of the way. The room only fits 75 people, and we are a tad bit more than that, so a few unlucky people may have to wait outside.

11:30–12:30 — Kanal tour

  • After the ceremony we rush to Gammel Strand (next to the metro), where we will be picked up by a Nettobåd, which will sail us to the venue. There will bubbles, snacks, and sodas on board. Click here for a song describing the anticipated experience. If you are not planning on joining for the boat ride, please let us know.

12:30-15:00 - Own time

  • This is a little awkward, but we couldn’t get our hoped-for time slot at City Hall, and we couldn’t book the boat in the afternoon. So, you’ll have some free time before the party starts. Louisa and I will use the time to take some pictures and get the venue ready, but you can use your time however you like. Get some lunch, relax, have another drink, or whatever you fancy.

15:00–17:30 — Reception

  • Now it gets serious. We will have a reception at the venue, Nordhus. The address is Århusgade 124A, 2150 København, Danmark.

17:30–21:30 — Dinner

  • Together with Nordhus, we have put together the most tasty menu you will have that day. Just to warn you: there are usually a lot of speeches and songs at Danish weddings, so the dinner may be on the longer side.

21:30–02:00 — Party

  • No more sitting down. It is time to dance. De Vilde Gardiner will kick of the party, followed by DJ 2low.

02:00 - Maybe we go out…

  • Who knows? Maybe we will continue the party in the city.

Sunday — 06/09/2026

12:30–15:00 (or later) — Chill at Nordhavn

  • We meet at Antwerpengade 15, 3. tv., 2150 Nordhavn. We are hoping for good weather, and if that hope is fulfilled, we will hang out at Jacob’s parents’ rooftop terrace, eat pizza, drink some soda (or beer), and have a good time. If we are very lucky with the weather, you can jump in the harbor for a swim — so bring your swim gear. If the weather sucks, we will come up with an alternative plan and let you know in due time.
